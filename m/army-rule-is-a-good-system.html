Army rule is a good system by country

People who say “having the army rule” is a very or fairly good way of governing their country. World Values Survey, or the European Values Study for most of Europe: about 1,000–3,000 people asked in each country, so figures are within about ±2–3 percentage points (the two studies sometimes differ by more). Don't-knows are left out. Latest survey from 2010 on.
