Willing to fight for their country by country

People who answer yes to “Of course, we all hope that there will not be another war, but if it were to come to that, would you be willing to fight for your country?” World Values Survey, or the European Values Study for most of Europe: about 1,000–3,000 people asked in each country, so figures are within about ±2–3 percentage points (the two studies sometimes differ by more). Don't-knows are left out. Latest survey from 2010 on.
