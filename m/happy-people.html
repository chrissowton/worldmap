Happy people by country

People who say they are very or quite happy, when asked: “Taking all things together, would you say you are very happy, quite happy, not very happy or not at all happy?” World Values Survey, or the European Values Study for most of Europe: about 1,000–3,000 people asked in each country, so figures are within about ±2–3 percentage points (the two studies sometimes differ by more). Don't-knows are left out. Latest survey from 2010 on.
