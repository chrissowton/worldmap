Taking a bribe: never justifiable by country

People who give 1 (“never justifiable”) on a 1–10 scale when asked whether someone accepting a bribe in the course of their duties can be justified. World Values Survey, or the European Values Study for most of Europe: about 1,000–3,000 people asked in each country, so figures are within about ±2–3 percentage points (the two studies sometimes differ by more). Don't-knows are left out. Latest survey from 2010 on.
