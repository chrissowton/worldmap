Resilience index (0–100) by country

The World Risk Poll's measure of how well people, households, communities and societies could cope with and recover from a shock such as a disaster. Higher is more resilient. World Risk Poll (Lloyd's Register Foundation, run by Gallup): about 1,000 people per country, every two years since 2019. Don't-knows are left out.
