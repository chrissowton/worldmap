Years under European colonial rule by country

How many years the land of today's country was a colony (or protectorate or mandate) of Belgium, Britain, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal or Spain, from the COLDAT dataset. 0 means none of these eight ruled it; some were ruled by other powers instead (such as the Ottoman, Russian or Japanese empires, Denmark or the USA), which this does not count.
