Would not want heavy drinkers as neighbours by country

People who pick heavy drinkers from a list of groups they would not like to have as neighbours. A widely used measure of tolerance. World Values Survey, or the European Values Study for most of Europe: about 1,000–3,000 people asked in each country, so figures are within about ±2–3 percentage points (the two studies sometimes differ by more). Don't-knows are left out. Latest survey from 2010 on.
