Which Countries?

What's new

Which Countries? is free and open source, and it keeps growing. These are the recent updates, newest first. Press Ctrl+Shift+R (⌘⇧R on a Mac) if the site doesn't show the latest version.

Version 57 November 2026

  • Income mountains (Charts → Income mountains): how many people live on each income, stacked by region, from 1800 to 2040, with the extreme poverty line and Gapminder's four income levels. Press play to watch the world move out of poverty.
  • How certain are population projections? Charts of population, life expectancy and fertility (with projections) now show the UN's 80% and 95% ranges as shaded bands for any country you add, and for the world and continents' population.
  • UN population projections now draw as smooth lines with world and continent totals.

Version 56

  • Regions within countries: the 🗺️ Regions button colours about 1,800 regions in 188 countries by the Human Development Index (every year from 1990), life expectancy, schooling, income and gender measures, with figures for women and men. See the highest and lowest regions, the countries with the biggest gaps between regions, every region of one country, and one region in detail. Data: Global Data Lab.
  • Faster start: the main data starts downloading straight away, while the rest of the page loads.
  • This page.

Version 55

  • Small multiples: one small chart for each continent or country, side by side.
  • Worldview quiz: questions about the world that most people get wrong, worked out from the latest data.
  • Describe now works for every chart, not just the map.
  • Notes: draw arrows, circles, highlights and text on any chart or map before downloading it.
  • Questions for students: teachers can add up to five questions to a shared link; students answer on the page.
  • Dollar Street links for every country.

Version 54

  • About this data for every chart and the map: definitions, sources, licences, years, coverage and how to cite.
  • A page for every measure, with every country ranked.
  • Embed any chart or map in a website or Google Site.
  • Line charts can show the change since a start year (or % change, or an index).
  • Bar chart race for any yearly measure.

Versions 50–53

  • Every chart has Download and Full screen buttons; country names or codes can be shown on charts.
  • The bubble chart fits the countries shown, has a title, a trend line and an option to show only matching countries.
  • Clearer filter editor (Add this filter / Done), colour-coded sections, resizable pop-ups, map boxes in the bottom-left corner, and side columns that close.
  • Statistics: plain-words summaries, explanations for every figure, sortable tables, and amber and red for spread that stands out.
  • Your own index can be used in every chart.

Versions 44–49

  • Climate graphs for thousands of weather stations, migration flows, city growth from 1975 to 2050, and about 50 new measures (corruption, press freedom, conflict deaths, child marriage, tourism, energy and more).
  • Quiz maker for teachers, Describe this map, a statistics toolkit (Spearman's rank, chi-squared, Lorenz curves), exam topics, guided tours, live hazards, a colour-blind mode, keyboard use and a table view.
